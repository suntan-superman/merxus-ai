AI Receptionist for Real Estate: Managing Calls and Capturing Property Inquiries

When agents are at a showing, on the road, or helping another client, a caller may still have a question about a property or want to request a showing. A configured AI receptionist can answer inbound calls, capture the inquiry, and give the team a clear follow-up path.

A practical coverage gap

Calls arrive while agents are doing agent work.

Real estate calls often come during showings, travel between appointments, open-house preparation, or after the office has closed. Sending every call to voicemail can leave a buyer or seller without an easy way to share what they need. Merxus is designed to answer configured inbound calls and capture the caller’s interest so a person can review the request and decide what happens next.

Missed calls

Give callers a way to explain why they called while the agent is occupied or unavailable.

Property inquiries

Capture which address or listing the caller means and what they want to know, using details the office has configured.

Showing interest

Record a showing request or preferred next step for an agent to confirm and coordinate.

What a useful call record includes

Capture enough context for a human follow-up.

Configure a concise intake around the information agents need to respond. Depending on the workflow, this may include the caller’s name and callback number, the property or listing they are asking about, whether they are buying or selling, the question they asked, and whether they want to request a showing. The exact fields and call flow should match the office’s configured process.

  • Caller name and preferred callback information.
  • Property address, listing reference, or other detail that identifies the inquiry.
  • Buyer, seller, showing, or general office intent where the caller shares it.
  • The caller’s question and the next step they requested.
  • Any request for a person to follow up, reviewed promptly by the team.

Collect only details the office needs. Agents should check the record, confirm facts, and decide who owns the response.

A simple office workflow

From incoming call to agent-owned next step.

  1. Answer consistently

    Use the office’s approved greeting and instructions for listing, buyer, seller, and general calls.

  2. Capture the property question

    Ask for the information needed to identify the property and understand the caller’s request without implying an answer the system cannot verify.

  3. Route the request

    Use the team’s configured workflow and alerts so the right person can review the call details in the existing Merxus web or mobile experience.

  4. Confirm and follow up

    An agent checks listing facts, availability, pricing, and any requested showing time, then contacts the caller and records the next step.

Set boundaries clearly

Use configured facts and keep agents in the loop.

Listing information can change. Only give the receptionist information the office has chosen to configure and can keep current. If an answer depends on live availability, a seller’s instructions, negotiation, financing, disclosures, or professional judgment, capture the question and route it to an agent. Do not imply a showing is booked, a property is still available, or a price is current until a person confirms it.

Merxus supports call answering and inquiry capture; this guide does not claim a live MLS connection, guaranteed lead conversion, or a replacement for agent advice. Offices should test their own instructions and escalation path before relying on any call workflow.

Make it fit your team

Keep your existing follow-up process in view.

Agree on which calls need an immediate person, how showing requests are confirmed, who reviews new inquiries, and how after-hours requests are handled the next business day. Use your own call patterns to decide which hours and call types to configure. If a caller raises an urgent or sensitive matter, follow the office’s human escalation instructions.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can Merxus answer questions about a property?

Merxus can help answer listing questions using the business workflow and information configured for it. If a caller asks about details that are not configured or may have changed, the safer next step is to capture the question and have an agent confirm it.

What information can a real estate office capture on a call?

The call flow can capture the caller’s contact details, the property or listing they are asking about, their reason for calling, and a requested next step such as showing interest. Agents should review the captured information and follow up.

Does an AI receptionist replace an agent or connect to live MLS availability?

No. Merxus supports call answering and inquiry capture; this guide does not claim a live MLS connection or guaranteed current availability. Agents remain responsible for confirming listing details, availability, advice, and next steps.
