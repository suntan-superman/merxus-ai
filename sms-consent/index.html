SMS Consent & Messaging Disclosure

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Merxus AI Transactional SMS Notifications

Merxus AI may send account and service-related SMS messages when a messaging workflow is enabled and the recipient is eligible to receive them. The working account registration and onboarding application is at merxusllc.com/onboarding.

This page does not collect SMS consent. The opt-in example illustrates sample disclosure wording. It is non-operational: it does not subscribe anyone or create a consent record. It must not be used as proof of opt-in or of an approved A2P campaign.

Creating an account does not, by itself, enroll a person in SMS messages. The actual permission and eligibility for each messaging workflow must be verified in the operational system before a message is sent.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

  • Message frequency varies based on account activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.

The example page does not collect a phone number, timestamp, IP address, account reference, or consent choice. Consent and opt-out records for an actual messaging workflow must be checked in the operational system.

Merxus AI does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers for marketing purposes.
